# Security policy

Please report security vulnerabilities privately to **security@holixplay.com**. Do not open a public issue.

Include the affected component and version, steps to reproduce and the impact. We acknowledge reports within 3 business days and keep you informed until the issue is fixed. We are happy to credit reporters once a fix is released.

This policy covers the Holix Play apps, holixplay.com and all repositories in the [HolixPlay](https://github.com/HolixPlay) organization. Machine-readable contact: <https://holixplay.com/.well-known/security.txt>.
